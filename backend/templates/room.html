    How it goes

    The rules of Classic. Every mode changes some of them — the card above says which.

    1. Everyone starts on the home base in the middle of the house.
    2. One player is the seeker. They shut their eyes and count to twenty.
    3. Everybody else runs and hides. Stay near the base and you get moved out — that is not hiding.
    4. Tuck into furniture to vanish. The seeker has to walk up and search it to find you.
    5. Tagged players freeze on the spot. Any hider who is still free can run straight through them to get them up again — no waiting about.
    6. The base keeps you safe only while you are stood on it. Step off and you are fair game again — it is a place, not a prize.
    7. The seeker cannot set foot on it at all. It is the one patch of floor in the house that is yours.
    8. Hiders win by getting all of themselves onto the base at once. The seeker wins by freezing everyone, or by running out the clock. Nothing else ends a round.
    {# The controls, before people are dropped into a dark house rather than after. Every row names both the key and the on-screen button, because half a room is on a laptop and half is on a phone and neither half can see the other's. #}

    Controls

    • WASD or ↑←↓→ Move. On a phone, the round pad on the left.
    • Shift or B Run. The bar at the bottom of the screen runs out, and empties into a walk until it comes back.
    • Y or Y Shout. The only thing in the house that goes through walls. Anyone near enough hears roughly which way you are — including the seeker.

    The second keycap on each row is the on-screen button, bottom right, on a phone or tablet.
