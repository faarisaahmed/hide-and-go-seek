    How it goes

    The rules of Classic. Every mode changes some of them — the card above says which.

    1. Everyone starts on the home base in the middle of the house.
    2. One player is the seeker. They shut their eyes and count to twenty.
    3. Everybody else runs and hides. Stay near the base and you get moved out — that is not hiding.
    4. Tuck into furniture to vanish. The seeker has to walk up and search it to find you.
    5. Tagged players freeze on the spot. A hider who is still free can stand with them to thaw them.
    6. Hiders win by getting all of themselves back onto the base. The seeker wins by freezing everyone.
